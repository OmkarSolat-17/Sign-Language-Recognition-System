# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level
of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

This project exists to reduce communication barriers; the community
around it should never create new ones. Hearing status and signing
ability are never a basis for exclusion — Deaf and hard-of-hearing
contributors' perspectives on sign language are especially valued and
should be centred in discussions about signs, datasets and UX.

## Our standards

**Positive behaviour:** welcoming and inclusive language; respect for
differing viewpoints and experience; graceful acceptance of constructive
criticism; focusing on what is best for the community; empathy towards
other members.

**Unacceptable behaviour:** sexualised language or imagery; trolling,
insulting/derogatory comments and personal or political attacks; public
or private harassment; publishing others' private information without
explicit permission; other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate
in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour
may be reported to the project maintainers via a GitHub issue (or
privately through GitHub's reporting tools). All complaints will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers may remove,
edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions
that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently
ban contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening,
offensive, or harmful.
